Nine bodies, nine strikes

Every sound on the board is one strike on the material the row is named for. The prompt is a physical description of that strike, and what changes from body to body is what the fire did to it: how hot it went, and so how long it rings.

every prompt has the same four parts: what is struck with what how it sounds, and for how long the room: close-miked, one hit, then silence the don'ts
clay
unfired, still wet. all transient, no body to ring

One single slap on a large lump of wet unfired clay with a flat palm. A dead, muffled, low thud with absolutely no ring and no resonance. Very short. Close-miked in a dry studio, one hit only, then silence. No music, no reverb, no room tone.
