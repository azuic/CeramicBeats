Ceramic Beats

A step sequencer built out of The Met’s open collection. Each of the nine tracks is a material the museum’s own catalogue names, and every tile you strike is a real object of that material, cropped from its photograph — 44,354 of them to draw from.

The sounds are strikes on the nine bodies themselves, generated with the ElevenLabs sound-effects API from a physical description of each fired body being hit. What separates them is what separates the materials: how hot each was fired, and so how long it rings. Unfired clay is dead, terracotta thuds, and the vitrified bodies — porcelain, faience, fritware — ring like the glass they very nearly are.
